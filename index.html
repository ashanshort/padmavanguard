About Us

Who We Are

Padma Vanguard Constructors is a fully licensed construction and civil engineering firm headquartered in Mirpur, Dhaka. Since 2012, we have been building quality structures for residential, commercial, and industrial clients across Bangladesh.

Our team of BUET-trained engineers, site managers, and skilled workers follow Bangladesh National Building Code (BNBC) standards on every project — with zero compromise on safety or quality.

  • Residential building construction
  • Commercial & office buildings
  • Industrial & factory construction
  • Renovation & interior finishing
  • Road & civil infrastructure
  • Project management & supervision
